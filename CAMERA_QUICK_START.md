# Camera System - Quick Start Guide

## 🎬 5-Minute Quick Start

### Basic Camera Controls
Open the **📷 CAMERA CONTROLS** section in the UI.

**Camera Modes:**
- **✈ Free Fly**: Full 6DOF movement (WASD + mouse)
- **◎ Follow**: Orbits around spirograph
- **🎬 AUTO ORBIT**: Automatic orbit around entire structure

---

## 🚀 Quick Presets

**Want a quick cinematic shot?**

1. Click **Camera Controls** section
2. Select a preset from dropdown:
   - **Fly-Around**: Circles the pattern
   - **Zoom In**: Dramatic approach
   - **Dolly Zoom**: Vertigo effect
   - **Reveal**: High-angle reveal
   - **Top-Down View**: Bird's eye
   - **Spiral In**: Spiral approach
   - **Figure-8**: Infinity path
3. Click **▶ PLAY**

**Stop anytime:** Click **⏹ STOP**

---

## 📍 Quick Camera Path

**Record a custom path:**

1. Position camera where you want
2. Click **+ WAYPOINT** (repeat 3-5 times)
3. Click **▶ PLAY PATH**

**Clear path:** Click **✖ CLEAR**

---

## 🎥 Quick Recording Setup

**For clean video recording:**

1. Set up your scene and agents
2. Toggle **🎥 Recording Mode** ← This hides all UI
3. Start your screen recorder
4. Play a preset or camera path
5. Stop recording
6. Toggle **Recording Mode** OFF to restore UI

---

## 🎯 Auto Features

**Smart camera adjustments:**

- **🎯 Auto-Framing**: Camera zooms to fit all agents ← Great for 2+ agents
- **👥 Multi-Agent Focus**: Tracks center of all agents ← Use with Auto-Framing
- **📳 Camera Shake**: Adds subtle organic movement ← Very subtle, adds life

**Recommended combo:**
- Enable Auto-Framing ✓
- Enable Multi-Agent Focus ✓
- Enable Camera Shake (keep it subtle!)

---

## ⚡ Pro Tips

1. **Smooth Recordings**: Use presets or waypoints (better than manual camera)
2. **Multi-Agent Setup**: Enable Auto-Framing + Multi-Agent Focus
3. **Subtle Shake**: Keep shake at 0.01-0.02 for natural feel
4. **Recording**: Always use Recording Mode to hide UI
5. **Custom Paths**: 4-6 waypoints work best for smooth paths

---

## 🎓 Example Workflow

**Professional recording in 1 minute:**

1. Spawn 4-8 agents (Multi-Agent section)
2. Open Camera Controls
3. Enable **Auto-Framing** ✓
4. Enable **Multi-Agent Focus** ✓
5. Enable **Camera Shake** (subtle) ✓
6. Select **Spiral In** preset
7. Toggle **Recording Mode** ✓
8. Start OBS/recorder
9. Click **▶ PLAY** preset
10. Record 5 seconds
11. Stop recorder
12. Toggle **Recording Mode** OFF

**Done!** You have a professional cinematic shot.

## 🔑 Keyboard Shortcuts

**Free Fly Mode:**
- **WASD / ZQSD**: Move
- **Space**: Up
- **Ctrl**: Down
- **Shift**: Sprint (faster)
- **Right Mouse**: Look around
- **Middle Mouse**: Pan
- **Scroll**: Zoom

**Any Mode:**
- **Enter**: Toggle UI visibility
- **Right Click** (in orbit): Exit auto-orbit

---

## 🐛 Quick Fixes

**Camera not moving?**
→ Check you're in the right mode (Free Fly for manual control)

**Preset not playing?**
→ Stop current preset first, then play new one

**UI disappeared?**
→ Press **Enter** or toggle Recording Mode off

**Camera too jerky?**
→ Reduce camera shake amount or disable it

**Can't see all agents?**
→ Enable Auto-Framing
